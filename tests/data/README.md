# Test data

- `vicmap_10m_z15_20112_29614.lerc`: one elevation tile (zoom 15, row 20112, column 29614: the Dandenong Ranges) from Vicmap Elevation DEM 10m, as served by Vicmap as a Service. Copyright (c) The State of Victoria, Department of Energy, Environment and Climate Action, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Unmodified. It checks that the LERC decoding works wherever the tests run.
- `kerb_widths_holdout.json`: the kerb-to-kerb calibration's hold-out (`core.kerbs.kerb_to_kerb`, 25 Nov 2026). Along every OSM town road (the kerb rule's: town speed, 8 or more building footprints within 80 m) in the City of Hobart's and Glenorchy's areas, every 2 m, the councils' kerb faces either side were found as `core.kerbs._council` finds them; the streets were split at random, 70% to fit the rule and 30% held out. This file is the held-out 30%, as aggregates only: per group of the tags the rule reads (and whether the stations are CBD-dense) its metres of road, and per class the measured median kerb to kerb. Derived from the City of Hobart's "Road Formation" ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)), "Glenorchy City Council Kerbs" ([CC BY 3.0 AU](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/au/)), OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/)) and theLIST's "LIST 2D Building Polygons" and cadastral parcels (© State of Tasmania, [CC BY 3.0 AU](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/au/)).
